14 reactions — how to trigger them
  • Time out — referee's T: one hand flat on top, one upright underneath
  • Heart — both hands, index tips together, thumb tips together
  • Cover nose — hands over your nose and mouth (one flat hand works too)
  • Crashing out — both hands on your head, mouth open
  • Dance — both hands up behind your head, mouth closed
  • Nose closed — pinch your nose shut
  • Flirty — one index fingertip on your lips
  • Hand up — one open palm up beside your head
  • Tongue out — mouth open, tongue out
  • Gasp — jaw drops
  • Disgusted — scrunch your nose and raise your upper lip, or brows down and frown
  • Talking to the wall — wave a hand back and forth, like you're arguing
  • Suspicious — turn your head and squint
  • Spin — leave the frame entirely
